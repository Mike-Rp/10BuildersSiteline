# Engineer credentials and assignment eligibility

## Deployment

1. Back up and review existing credential and specialization records using the read-only audit below.
2. Apply migrations through 014, then `Thesis-Backend/migrations/015-engineer-credentials.sql` using your normal reviewed deployment process. The migration is repeatable; tested in isolated PGlite only. No live migration was performed.
3. Deploy backend and frontend together and restart the API. Do not run the updated account/workspace queries against the old schema. Until migration, new assignment eligibility fails closed rather than accepting uploaded files.
4. Review legacy credentials and explicitly record specializations before making new assignments. Existing assignments, approvals and historical records are not removed.

## Entering accounts

Create an Office PM or Site PM account normally. Office PM project-type tags retain their existing purpose. Account creation grants account access, not professional eligibility. Open the account's Professional credentials section, select each licensed profession, enter the exact number and expiry, and upload a PDF/JPG/PNG. Administrator uploads use **Save credential** and immediately record verified evidence with the Administrator's identity and save time. Engineer submissions through My Profile remain pending for Administrator review.

For engineer-submitted evidence, an Administrator records the review result and verifies or requests correction. Direct Administrator saves do not require a second review step or create a review notification. Updates supersede only the same profession; previous evidence and review history are retained. A pending submission replaced by an Administrator save is closed with that reason. Reviewer identity and timestamp are generated by the server. This is not PRC integration or an automated competence assessment.

Record up to three engineer specializations independently of company trades. The system records who entered them and when; this is attribution, not a claim that competence was independently certified.

## Professional scope and mappings

Supported credential professions: Civil Engineer, Mechanical Engineer, Electrical Engineer, Sanitary Engineer, Electronics Engineer, Geodetic Engineer. These labels are application categories, not a complete classification of license grades or signing authorities. Verify the exact credential and responsibility manually, especially distinctions within Electrical and Mechanical engineering.

Structural and Geotechnical specializations map to Civil Engineer. Mechanical, Electrical, Sanitary, Electronics and Surveying map to their corresponding professional categories. Plumbing phase requirements map to a Sanitary specialization for engineer site coordination. Interior/Finishes and Landscape phase labels currently map to Civil site coordination; this does not confer architectural, interior-design or landscape-design authority. The company must validate these operational coordination mappings before rollout.

Fire Protection, Facade and Acoustic specializations may be recorded, but have no qualifying-profession mapping and cannot grant eligibility until professionally/company validated. No professional credential is inferred from legacy tags.

A phase match means the person can be considered for their matching site coordination responsibility. It does not mean one engineer is authorized to sign or oversee every technical discipline listed in that phase. Phase templates and the existing partial-match staffing approach remain unchanged. The company must separately validate whether its staffing model sufficiently covers each professional responsibility.

Sources reviewed November 1, 2026:
- [PRC public verification](https://verification.prc.gov.ph/): public searches by profession/name or profession/license number/birthdate. Does not establish a universal license-number length.
- [PRC Civil Engineer primer](https://www.prc.gov.ph/uploaded/documents/Civil%20Engineer_PRIMER.pdf): identifies structural/geotechnical and construction-related areas. Not a substitute for current profession-specific legal advice or project authority review.

## Number format

No authoritative universal or supported-profession-specific digit length was established from the reviewed sources. The code therefore enforces a 1–120-character string input bound, rejects control characters and leading/trailing whitespace, and preserves leading zeros and punctuation. This is an input safety bound, not a claimed PRC number format. Never infer authenticity from this check. Numeric JSON input is rejected. No existing number is truncated or rewritten; questionable legacy records require review.

## Independent credential lifecycle

Engineer submission/rejection does not replace the existing credential. Administrator verification or a direct Administrator save replaces current records of that profession only. A legacy record is superseded only after the explicit Administrator action succeeds. Historical records remain available. Unknown legacy professions are never inferred; historical verified renewal attribution is retained, while unknown profession still prevents new assignment qualification.

Credential verification and expiry are independent. Account Active does not mean a valid credential. The existing 30-day notice restriction remains for new assignments; valid future expiry during a project remains a warning. One expired credential does not invalidate another credential. Suggestions and assignment writes use verified profession and recorded compatible specialization, plus existing availability and holds. Ongoing assignment policy has not been replaced: old account-wide write restrictions choose a reviewed credential with the latest expiry for display/access compatibility. Existing assignments do not carry a per-credential binding, so mixed-profession ongoing responsibilities need company review rather than automatic reassignment.

## Read-only audit

Run `Thesis-Backend/scripts/audit-engineer-credentials.sql` only after migration. It lists ambiguous current records and active phase assignments without current qualification coverage. It does not repair, remove or reassign anything. Review results with the company, including Office PM credential and project-type coverage.

## Scope

This change does not fix missing-phase activation, redesign phases, change office appointment signing or deploy anything. Existing custom preview files are untouched.
