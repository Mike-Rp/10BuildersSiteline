# Client photo examples

Three construction examples were generated with the built-in imagegen tool for the Client photo collage. They are frontend assets, not actual site uploads or project evidence. The sample gallery appears only for the active `gonzales.vialh@10builders.com` account, only within its existing authorized project view, and only when that project has no released site photos. Actual shared photos always take precedence. Samples do not change photo counts, progress, dates, evidence, notifications or backend records.

The compact gallery uses one large left tile and two smaller stacked right tiles. Both samples and actual authorized uploads can be opened using their existing or clearly labeled sample preview. Sample previews do not call document APIs or create activity records.

## Generated assets and final prompts

Built-in image generation was used, with no CLI fallback. The selected PNG outputs were encoded as WebP for web delivery without changing their dimensions or composition. All prompts requested single photorealistic fictional-site images, natural daylight, realistic construction materials, no people, logos, text, collage or watermark.

- `public/images/client-sample-foundation.webp`: A portrait documentary photograph of a fictional mixed-use building foundation under construction in Metro Manila, seen from the site boundary. Rebar columns rise from concrete footings, with organized formwork, gravel access and a partially built reinforced concrete frame. Natural overcast daylight, realistic materials and a neutral blue-gray concrete palette. Intended for a tall Client gallery tile. Illustrative example only, not evidence of a real project.
- `public/images/client-sample-reinforcement.webp`: A landscape close documentary photograph of neatly tied reinforcing steel in a concrete foundation beam, timber formwork and concrete footings at a fictional mixed-use construction site in Metro Manila. Soft daylight, realistic construction details, gray concrete and warm timber. Intended for a small wide gallery tile. Illustrative fictional-site sample only.
- `public/images/client-sample-slab.webp`: A landscape documentary photograph across a reinforced concrete ground-level foundation slab and column starter bars, with clean timber formwork, unfinished concrete columns and a cloudy tropical sky at a fictional mixed-use construction site in Metro Manila. Natural daylight, understated colors and rough concrete textures. Illustrative fictional-site sample only.

The layout reference supplied by the user guided the HTML/CSS collage arrangement, not the content of the generated construction photos.
